Academic Identity

About SAS

The School of Arts and Sciences is an academic community dedicated to developing students through holistic education, intellectual formation, values development, and meaningful service.

Through its academic programs, faculty, student organizations, and co-curricular activities, SAS provides opportunities for students to grow academically, personally, socially, and professionally.

The School of Arts and Sciences Student Portal serves as a centralized digital information hub designed to make important student services, announcements, attendance information, organizational details, and academic references easier to access.
